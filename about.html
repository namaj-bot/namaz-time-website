About the app

A clear, simple way to check prayer times.

Namaz Time is being developed for Android users who want a straightforward daily prayer-time companion.

⌖

GPS-based calculation

The app requests location permission to calculate prayer times for the device's current location. If permission is denied, location-based calculation may not be available.

◷

Calculation settings

The current project configuration uses the Karachi calculation method and Hanafi madhab setting for Asr.

文

Bengali and English

The app is planned to support both Bengali and English interfaces.

✦

Planned additions

Next-prayer countdown, prayer notifications, monthly calendar, Qibla compass and offline schedule support are planned. They are not available until included in a published release.

Development status

Namaz Time is still in development. It has not been verified as production-ready and is not yet available on Google Play. Features may change before release.
