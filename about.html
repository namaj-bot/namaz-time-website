ABOUT NAMAZ TIME

A simple companion for your daily prayers.

Namaz Time is an Android prayer-time app in development, designed to help users check daily prayer times based on their device location.

⌖

GPS-Based Location

The app is designed to use device location permission to calculate prayer times for the user's current location. Location access depends on the permission granted by the user.

◷

Karachi Calculation Method

The current project configuration uses the Karachi method for calculating prayer times.

☪

Hanafi Asr

The current project configuration uses the Hanafi madhab setting for calculating Asr prayer time.

文

Bengali and English

The app is being designed with Bengali and English language support to make it accessible to more users.

FUTURE PLANS

Features planned for the future

Next-prayer countdown, prayer notifications, monthly prayer calendar, Qibla compass and offline schedule support are being considered for future development. These features are not confirmed as available in the current version.

DEVELOPMENT STATUS

Currently in development

Namaz Time is still under development and is not yet available on Google Play. Features and functionality may change before the official release.
