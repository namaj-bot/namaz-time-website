Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This page describes the current Namaz Time Android starter project and this official website. The Android app is still under development. This policy must be reviewed and updated before release if the final app adds analytics, advertising, crash reporting, cloud services or other third-party SDKs.

1. Location permission

The Android starter manifest declares both approximate (coarse) and precise (fine) location permissions. The app requests a high-accuracy current position for prayer-time calculation. Android may still let a user grant approximate location only. You can manage or revoke location permission in Android settings. If permission is denied or location services are off, location-based prayer-time calculation may not work.

2. How the app uses location

In the starter source reviewed for this draft, the app obtains the device location and calculates prayer times on the device using the Karachi calculation method and Hanafi Asr setting. The app screen also displays the obtained latitude and longitude. The reviewed source contains no app account or developer-operated server endpoint for uploading those coordinates. This statement describes the starter source only; the complete release build and all included dependencies must be checked again before publication.

3. Other information and third-party services

The reviewed starter project does not implement account registration, advertising, analytics, crash reporting or a developer backend. The final app may behave differently if such services are added. Before release, any added service and the information it receives must be disclosed in this policy and in the applicable Google Play Data safety disclosures. Android, Google Play and device services may separately process information under their own terms.

4. This website

This static website has no contact form, analytics, advertising code, remote scripts, external fonts or embedded third-party widgets in this version. It does not request your browser's location. Its Bengali/English language switch saves your selected language (bn or en) in your browser's local storage so the preference can be remembered on other pages; the website code does not send that preference to the developer. The email link opens your own email application. If you choose to send an email, your email provider will process the message and the developer will receive it. The hosting provider may process ordinary technical request information, such as connection data, under its own terms.

5. Data retention and security

The reviewed starter source does not implement developer-operated server storage for location data. Location permission can be changed or revoked in Android settings. The website language preference remains in your browser's local storage until you clear that browser data or the site changes it. If future app versions store information locally or use online services, this policy will be updated to explain the data, purpose and retention period.

6. Children's privacy

Namaz Time is a general prayer-time utility and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. If the app's intended audience or features change, relevant child-safety and store requirements will be reviewed before release.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app features or website data practices change. The latest revision date will appear at the top of this page.
